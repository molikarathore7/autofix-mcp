The `/customer-email` endpoint attempts to retrieve a customer's email using the dictionary key 'email_address', but the email is stored under the key 'email' when a customer is added.

2. Explain why it occurred in short one line.
There is a mismatch between the dictionary key used to store the email and the key used to retrieve it.

3. Suggest the fix in short one line.
Change the key from `customer["email_address"]` to `customer["email"]` in the `/customer-email` endpoint.
